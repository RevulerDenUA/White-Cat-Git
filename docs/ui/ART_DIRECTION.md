# White Cat visual direction

The station carries the scene. The controls borrow its ceramic shells, blue solar hardware and clipped module corners. The menu should feel like a space expedition game with tangible equipment.

Palette: space #080e17, ceramic #d4dedf, slate #1b2b38, white #eef0ec, cool selection #c6dae9. Warmth stays in sunlight and the station’s existing ochre materials.

Type: Barlow Condensed Semibold for the game wordmark, Onest for controls. No numbered menu items, tracked eyebrows, marketing headings or fictitious readings.

Layout: title upper left, one substantial ceramic Start control lower left, two smaller equipment-style controls beneath it. The station occupies the right-hand 3D scene. Button shapes and the station pictogram are drawn specifically for this project. Focus and press states belong to the controls and have no decorative rails or appended arrows.

Calibration: Homeworld’s scene-first composition, the equipment language of space-work games, and Outer Wilds’ coherent stylised world. Keep these influences selective, without importing another game’s assets or reproducing its screen.

The Start action has one continuous 3D camera path, parallax and a far-focus change. Reduced motion replaces it with a short fade.
